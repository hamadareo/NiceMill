## 2. The tracking engine (separate code base, NOT in this repository)

The engine that the app calls as a subprocess installs its own environment, which includes:

| Package | Licence | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **ultralytics** (YOLO11) | **AGPL-3.0** (an Ultralytics Enterprise licence is sold separately) | Strong copyleft. Distributing the engine, or offering it as a network service, requires releasing the corresponding source under AGPL-3.0 or buying the Enterprise licence. |
| lap | BSD-2-Clause | checked 2026-11-23: LICENSE of github.com/gatagat/lap (BSD 2-Clause) |
| av | BSD-3-Clause (bundles LGPL FFmpeg) | |

The tools that produce pose, ball-detection and action-check cue files for the review player depend on `ultralytics`
(AGPL-3.0). **Because of this licence they are not included in this repository.** The review player's `--pose-file`,
`--ball` and `--claim-cues` inputs are optional; without those files the corresponding features are simply off.
`scripts/setup_pfb3x3_engine.sh` only installs `ultralytics` into the separate engine environment on the user's own machine.
