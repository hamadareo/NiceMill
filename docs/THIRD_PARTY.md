## 3. Known blockers

- **ultralytics (YOLO11) + `yolo11l.pt` weights: AGPL-3.0** (engine venv, `engine/pfb3x3.py` detector). Distributing it, or offering it as a network service, requires releasing the source under AGPL or buying the Ultralytics Enterprise licence. Not removed in this lane; decision pending (buy vs. swap to RTMDet / YOLOX / D-FINE, Apache-2.0).
- NVIDIA CUDA wheels pulled by torch / vllm are under NVIDIA's proprietary redistribution terms (redistribution allowed with the notices; not OSI open source).
- Model weights (Qwen3-VL, faster-whisper / large-v3-turbo) and fonts are not Python packages and are not scanned here: Apache-2.0 / MIT / OFL; keep their notices.
